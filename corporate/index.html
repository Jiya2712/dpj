Areas of Practice

Corporate

Advisory · Transactions · Documentation

We advise businesses, founders and investors on corporate and commercial matters, from day-to-day governance and documentation to mergers, acquisitions, investments, and capital-markets transactions.

General Corporate & Commercial

Our corporate commercial practice supports businesses through everyday legal needs and structural changes alike, including mergers, amalgamations, and corporate restructuring. We advise on entity formation, governance, contracts, and regulatory compliance, helping clients run their operations smoothly while ensuring that mergers and internal reorganisations are executed lawfully, efficiently, and with minimal disruption.

Mergers and Acquisitions

We guide clients through the full lifecycle of mergers and acquisitions, from deal structuring and due diligence to negotiation, documentation, and closing. Our team advises on share and asset purchases, business transfers, and post-merger integration, helping buyers, sellers, and investors navigate complex transactions while protecting value and managing legal and commercial risk throughout.

Private Equity and Venture Capital

We advise start-ups, investors, and funds on private equity and venture capital transactions, including funding rounds, term sheets, shareholder agreements, and exit strategies. Our support spans due diligence, regulatory compliance, and negotiation, helping founders raise capital on fair terms while giving investors confidence through sound structuring and clear, enforceable investment documentation.

Capital Markets

Our capital markets practice assists companies and intermediaries with public and private fundraising, including IPOs, rights issues, and debt offerings. We advise on regulatory compliance under securities laws, listing requirements, and disclosure obligations, helping clients navigate the regulatory approval process while structuring offerings that meet both business objectives and market regulations.

Corporate Documentation (MoU, AoA, etc.)

We draft and review foundational corporate documents including memoranda of understanding, articles of association, and shareholder agreements, ensuring they accurately reflect commercial intent and legal requirements. Our documentary advisory support helps businesses establish clear governance frameworks and rights from the outset, reducing the likelihood of future disputes among founders, partners, and investors.
