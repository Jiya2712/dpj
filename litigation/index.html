Areas of Practice

Litigation

Comprehensive Litigation, Documentation & Advisory Services

We represent clients before District Courts, High Courts, the Supreme Court, and tribunals across criminal, civil, matrimonial, commercial, tax, intellectual property, and service matters, and support them with documentation and advice that helps prevent disputes.

Criminal Litigation

Criminal litigation deals with offences against the law and society, from FIR to final appeal. Our firm represents clients in bail, anticipatory bail, quashing petitions, economic offences, cheque dishonour, cybercrime, and cases before investigating agencies, defending liberty and reputation with a strategy tailored to each stage of the criminal process.

Civil Litigation

Civil litigation resolves disputes between private parties seeking compensation, possession, or declaration of rights. We handle property and title disputes, recovery suits, partition, breach of contract, landlord-tenant matters, and RERA and consumer claims, pursuing both courtroom advocacy and arbitration or mediation for efficient, practical resolution.

Matrimonial Litigation

Matrimonial litigation addresses disputes arising from marriage and family relationships, handled with sensitivity and confidentiality. We represent clients in divorce, judicial separation, maintenance, alimony, custody, and domestic violence matters, striving for amicable settlement wherever possible while protecting our clients' rights firmly when litigation becomes necessary.

Intellectual Property Litigation

Intellectual property litigation protects trademarks, copyrights, patents, designs, and trade secrets that give businesses their competitive edge. We handle infringement, passing-off, and patent disputes, anti-counterfeiting enforcement, and licensing disagreements, while supporting IP due diligence and brand protection so our clients' innovations remain secure and enforceable.

Tax Law Litigation

Tax law litigation covers disputes between taxpayers and revenue authorities under income tax, GST, and customs law. We represent clients before appellate tribunals and courts in assessment, search, and penalty matters, while assisting with advance rulings and compliance strategy to reduce future litigation risk and financial exposure.

Commercial, Recovery & Cheque Bounce

This practice addresses financial disputes businesses face, from cheque dishonour under Section 138 to defaulted loans and breached agreements. We handle recovery suits, insolvency proceedings, Debt Recovery Tribunal matters, and SARFAESI actions, pursuing settlement or execution swiftly so clients recover what they are rightfully owed.

Service Litigation

Service litigation concerns disputes between employees or officers and their employers, especially in government service. We represent clients before Administrative Tribunals in disciplinary proceedings, wrongful termination, promotion, pension, and recruitment disputes, protecting career interests while guiding employers through lawful and fair disciplinary compliance.

Documentation & Advisory

Not every legal need leads to court, so we help clients avoid disputes through careful documentation and advice. We draft contracts, wills, agreements, and property documents, and advise on compliance, structuring, and risk, building a strong legal foundation so litigation remains the exception rather than the rule.
