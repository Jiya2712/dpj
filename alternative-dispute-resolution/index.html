Areas of Practice

Alternative Dispute Resolution

Arbitration · Mediation · Conciliation

Alternative dispute resolution offers a faster, confidential route to resolving disputes outside conventional litigation. We represent clients in arbitration, mediation, and conciliation proceedings, from the first step to an enforceable outcome.

Arbitration

We represent clients in domestic and international arbitration proceedings, from drafting arbitration clauses to appearing before tribunals and enforcing awards. Our practice covers ad hoc and institutional arbitration across commercial, construction, and shareholder disputes, offering clients a faster, confidential, and enforceable alternative to conventional litigation without compromising on advocacy or strategy.

Mediation

Our mediation practice helps parties resolve disputes through structured, facilitated negotiation rather than adversarial proceedings. We represent clients before court-annexed and private mediation centres, guiding them toward mutually acceptable solutions that preserve relationships and save time and cost, while ensuring their legal interests remain protected throughout the process.

Conciliation

We assist clients in conciliation proceedings, where a neutral conciliator helps parties reach a voluntary settlement, particularly in commercial, labour, and consumer disputes. Our role includes preparing settlement terms, representing clients before conciliation officers and forums, and ensuring that any resulting agreement is fair, enforceable, and properly documented.
